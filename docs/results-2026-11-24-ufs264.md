# ufs264 RAM runs and the readback channel (2026-11-24)

Two unattended `fastboot boot` runs of `boot-piano-ufs264cfg.img`
(SHA-256 `d74fad3a4b8425bbc3fe929c776a985eaa2a69aadba30770cc7baf63b1e83a4a`,
185,733,120 bytes) returned to fastboot by themselves: one at 37.3 s after the
`OKAY`, one at 51.9 s. Both are in the same window as the control images
without the timer change (ufs256 38.6 s, ufs263 44.9 s), so the hand-written
PPI 27 branch no longer clearing `CNTV_CTL_EL0` has not moved this death yet.
No partition, GPT or userdata write was made.

The readback channel works again, with two constraints learned here:

- Boot the maintenance image and the reader with platform-tools 33.0.3
  (`out/pt33`). fastboot 37.0.3 rejects
  `boot-ram-maintenance.img` with
  `Failed to load/authenticate boot image: Load Error`, while 33.0.3 sends and
  boots it (`OKAY`).
- One dump per reader boot. Issuing a second `oem dump` on the same reader
  session fails with `Status read failed (Too many links)` or
  `usb_read failed` and the device leaves fastboot. Read the slot table in one
  reader boot and the console in a second one; DRAM survives the intervening
  `fastboot reboot bootloader`.

An 8 KiB dump of PA `0x8c5912880` was retrieved (`ufs264-slots.bin`, 8192
bytes). Its entries are not the probe values: every code 0x0b-0xca holds a
distinct monotonically increasing 64-bit word (steps of `0x200000004` per
32-byte entry), the shape of a page-table walk rather than
`piano_dbg_kv()` copies. The address is the one derived for the ufs256 KC; the
ufs264 KC has its own `_piano_stage_slots` VA, so the dump cannot be decoded
until that VA is recomputed from the ufs264 kernel. Treat the slot contents as
unread, not as evidence that every probe ran.

The device was returned to HyperOS OS3.0.309.0.WPYCNXM on slot A with
`sys.boot_completed=1` after these runs.
