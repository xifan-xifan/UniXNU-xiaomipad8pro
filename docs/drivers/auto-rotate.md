# LT-C3 automatic display rotation

**B3 remains in RAM qualification. Automatic display rotation is off in the daily profile.** The [November 27 results](../results-2026-11-27.md#ram-qualification) record that `v131y12t-qual1` passed the accelerometer SSC sample-clock stream with `piano-sensors-accel-sample-time=1` and the `piano-rotated` boot-argument read past 1024 bytes (`RO1/RO2/RO6/BA3`). The remaining RAM checks cover automatic display rotation, the physical cover transitions, and rotation through suspend and wake.

Earlier RAM runs established the stand edge and first ALS target on `sens-island5`, manual 0° and 90° display rotation on `touch-native3`, and native touch at 90° using panel axes on `touch-native2`.

## End-to-end checks

| Step | Status | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LSM6DSO → ADSP SSC | **ours-OK (RAM)** | PianoPower owns the running ADSP. PianoSensors serves the sensor process's pack through FastRPC, discovers SSC service 0x190 and looks up the default `accel` SUID. See [sensors.md](sensors.md) §§3–5c. `sens-island5` verified request 513 and the first accelerometer vector. ALS request 514 shares the hub and uses its own client. |
| SSC → PianoSensors | **ours-OK (source, host, RAM bench)** | `SensorsStream.c` sends 513 at 10 Hz by default on its own QRTR port. QMI reports 0x21/0x22 carry a SUID and events. Event 1025 carries fixed64 QTimer ticks and at least three float components in m/s². The parser checks length, SUID and stream state before using a sample. Session restart, guard strike, sleep and the display gate are exercised by the host stream tests. Physical detach and reattach in ordinary acquisition mode are **unknown**. |
| Sample → candidate edge | **ours-OK (host, stand pose on RAM)** | `SensorsPolicy.c` converts the SSC up vector to fixed-point down gravity and applies `a+y+x+z`. With the default map, SSC +X, +Y, −X and −Y are top, left, bottom and right. Top is measured and the two side signs remain **unknown** on physical hardware. `SensorsOrient.c` accepts 0.6–1.4 g, requires a 1.3× leading-axis margin at diagonals, and after a 250 ms hold publishes a new state. A shock, free fall, nonfinite component or ambiguous diagonal breaks a candidate hold. A stable vector with at least about 85% of its magnitude on Z maps to `none` in either direction, leaving the display at its current angle. |
| Timing of the 250 ms hold | **ours-OK (host and RAM stream)** | With `piano-sensors-accel-sample-time=1`, event 1025 timestamps drive the hold on the 19.2 MHz SSC QTimer. Missing timestamps, backward jumps or wrap, and gaps over two requested sample periods (200 ms at 10 Hz) reset the candidate. Duplicates are ignored. A timing discontinuity retains the last accepted edge. Closing the acquisition gate or starting a new SSC session clears the edge and dwell. Receive time remains the default and gives every event in one batch the same time. Rotation across live s2idle still needs a RAM check. |
| Edge → `piano-rotated` | **ours-OK (source and host policy)** | `PianoSensors.cpp` publishes `piano-sensors-edge`, then `piano-sensors-seq`, and sends a property-change message on edge changes. The agent also polls the sequence every 250 ms when the panel is awake and the cover is detached (every 5 s when asleep). `AutoRotate.c` and `ps_rotation_step` require a further 600 ms of edge dwell and a 1 s minimum between rotations. Face-up/down or an observed display sleep resets the pending edge. The `Locked` preference blocks sensor-driven requests. A manual rotation holds until the edge changes. An overdue proposal waits for fresh input when the agent cannot apply it. Delivery in a real detached session is **unknown**. |
| Userland and profile | **ours-OK (host and RAM boot-argument read)** | The staged LoginWindow/Aqua LaunchAgent exits successfully before opening the display backend or watching drivers when `piano-rotate` is absent or 0. The implemented key is `piano-rotate`. The daemon and shell helper prefer the merged `kern.piano_bootargs` sysctl and fall back to `kern.bootargs` only if the merged OID is absent. Both readers use the last exact key. Level 1 observes, level 2 rotates the panel, and level 3 also sends the shown angle to PianoTouch. |
| Agent → WindowServer | **ours-OK (manual 0°/90° RAM)** | `piano-rotated.m` requires the internal panel’s vendor/model or an explicit diagnostic display override, checks the MonitorPanel `MPDisplay` method encodings and `canChangeOrientation`, then invokes `setOrientation:` from a live console session. `CGDisplayRotation` is the public read-back. The agent serializes display reconfiguration work on the main queue, refreshes cover and edge state before deciding, and verifies the angle within 2 s. It backs off on failure and gives up after five failures. Automatic requests through the agent and 180°/270° on the tablet are **unknown**. |
| WindowServer → scanout | **ours-OK (manual 90° RAM), unknown (rendering-angle call and rotated flip eligibility)** | The 27.3 decoded cache has MonitorPanel's `MPDisplay`, SkyLight's `SLSSetDisplayRotation`, CoreDisplay's `CGXSetDisplayRotation` and `IOMobileFramebufferSetRenderingAngle`. Prior symbol analysis in [sensors.md](sensors.md#5a1-display-rotation-the-mechanism-verdict) identifies the IOMobileFramebuffer family's inherited rendering-angle hook. `NativeGopDisplay.cpp` submits through that family and presents through its copy or PianoDpu flip path. The observed portrait desktop establishes that scanout works at 90°. The exact rendering-angle calls, swap surface layout and choice of copy/flip in each orientation still need runtime evidence. |
| Raw touch → panel axes → desktop | **ours-OK (host and 90° RAM), unknown (physical corners in all four rotations)** | `TouchMatrix.h` maps input to the fixed 3200×2136 panel. An explicit `piano-touch-flip` takes priority over the build's `PIANO_TOUCH_INVERT_Y`, then calibration, then panel defaults. The native Touch Screen binds to the internal display's `IOMFBUUID` and by default sends panel coordinates. BackBoard rotates them for the displayed desktop, as observed in `touch-native2`. Agent level 3 and `piano-touch-rotate=1` are for the separate desktop-axis path, where the agent sends only the read-back angle and PianoTouch latches it at the next gesture. A new gesture between display reconfiguration and the touch property write can still begin in the previous coordinate map. Use level 2 with native panel axes so BackBoard owns the desktop transform. See [touch.md](touch.md#187-the-binding-by-itself-and-the-axes-touch-native2-2026-11-23). |
| Keyboard cover → landscape | **ours-OK (source and host), unknown (physical attach from portrait)** | `PianoKeyboard.cpp` publishes the MCU attach bit as `piano-keyboard-attached` and notifies clients. The agent's default `piano-rotate-cover=2` returns to 0° subject to the minimum interval and holds landscape. The cover rule takes precedence over sensor lock. An in-flight WindowServer request can still show before the return to 0°. Detach applies a 1.5 s hold, then a fresh 600 ms agent settle, so a tablet held in portrait can rotate again once its edge is valid. The kernel normally stops acceleration while attached. The hall/lid input is distinct from the attach bit. A missing cover property counts as detached by default. |

The remote `sensors/rot1` branch contains the optional raw-vector property (`17d30be3`), manual-rotation notes (`c6651288`), and a foreground `--cover` override (`76af7f35`). Those commits are outside the baseline of this change. The existing boot controls and `TestEdge` provide the test path on this branch.

The reference uses SSC through libssc rather than an IIO buffer on this board. The inspected sheng checkout had no equivalent orientation path.

## Host verification

- `python3 scripts/ci/self-tests.py --only PianoAuto` covers all four SSC axis signs and display angles, the sensor and agent dwell boundaries, diagonal bounce, flat holds, shake and free fall, cover transitions during a pending request, and touch updates after display read-back. The daemon and shell tests use synthetic boot arguments to check exact-key selection, overrides after the legacy clip, default-off startup, and fallback when the merged sysctl is absent.
- `python3 package/PianoSensors/tests/run-host-tests.py` feeds synthetic QMI reports to the sensor engine. It covers receive-time and sample-time modes, batched events, missing and duplicate timestamps, the gap boundary, and fresh dwell after sleep and cover gating. The wake cases exercise continuous time, a forward jump and a backward reset independently of the AP receive clock.
- `package/PianoTouch/tests/touch_native_host.cpp` checks HID reports at all four corners for each angle in both coordinate modes. The host model ends at the HID reports. The optical scanout and BackBoard's desktop transform need RAM evidence.

## RAM qualification checklist

Use a volatile RAM boot with a timed return and an active console GUI session. Record the image identifier and revision, console and agent log windows, the staged sensor pack and kext identity, and `piano-rotated --probe` output. Check for the internal panel, `canChangeOrientation 1`, an awake desktop and display-bound native touch in panel axes. Record the relevant boot keys from `kern.piano_bootargs`, and keep captured output and C920 media in the run's private evidence.

### Unattended RAM checks

- [ ] **Default-off and boot-argument gate.** Boot the daily configuration in RAM once with `piano-rotate` absent and once with `piano-rotate=0`. With the LaunchAgent staged, use `launchctl print gui/<uid>/com.unixnu.piano-rotated` and the agent log to confirm exit status 0 and no restart, live rotation loop, automatic request or PianoTouch write. With `piano-sensors-rotate=1`, confirm there is no accelerometer consumer or request 513. Check that ordinary display, touch and configured ALS still work. For the positive control, boot with `piano-rotate-test=1`, place `piano-rotate=1` beyond the 1024-byte legacy boundary, and require the merged sysctl and `--probe` to agree on level 1. Inject a settled edge and require a logged proposal with unchanged display angle and no touch write.
- [ ] **Injected display path.** Boot `piano-rotate=2 piano-rotate-test=1 piano-rotate-cover=0`. As the console user, run `defaults write com.unixnu.piano-rotated TestEdge -int N` and `notifyutil -p com.unixnu.piano-rotated.prefs` per step. Use `0, 1, 2, 3, 0`, waiting at least 4 s per step, and expect read-back `0°, 90°, 180°, 270°, 0°`. Inject `-1` in portrait and require a hold. Alternate the two side edges faster than 600 ms, finish at `-1`, and require no new rotation. Correlate the test-edge marker, each request, CoreGraphics read-back and desktop bounds. Capture the C920 view if available. An optional run with `piano-touch-inject=1` can exercise the corner event path.
- [ ] **Cover-state gate with injected edges.** Start a separate injected run with `piano-keyboard=2`, the cover already attached, and the default `piano-rotate-cover=2`. Require `piano-keyboard-attached=1` in IORegistry and the agent's attached log. Inject 1, 2 and 3 and require 0° throughout. As a positive test of forced landscape, issue `piano-rotated --set 90` in the console session and require the agent to restore 0° after the minimum interval. A run with the cover already detached can demonstrate that the same inputs do rotate. The physical attach and detach transitions are in the attended pass.
- [ ] **Live SSC through s2idle.** With the cover detached, use `piano-sensors=4 piano-sensors-rotate=1 piano-rotate=2 piano-sensors-accel-sample-time=1` with native panel-axis touch and leave the tablet in a fixed upright pose. Arm the RAM timer-wake harness before each of 10 `pmset sleepnow` cycles. Verify s2idle entry and timer wake in the power log. Capture `piano-sensors-{streams,edge,seq}` from `ioreg -r -c PianoSensors -l` before and after each cycle. Keep the PianoSensors disable/re-enable logs and correlate the opt-in marker, request 513, first event 1025, session, advancing samples and edge. Compare the stream's `qtimer` reading just before sleep with the first post-wake sample's `qtimer` marker. Require the old edge to clear, no rotation during sleep or on a discontinuity alone, and a valid edge after fresh SSC dwell. The desktop should still match the fixed pose when the wakes settle. Record whether live QTimer continued, jumped or reset. The host replay exercises the discontinuities independently of what the live device does.
- [ ] **Stability.** Repeat at least 20 injected rotations and complete all 10 suspend/resume cycles in the opt-in profiles. Record the agent and WindowServer PIDs and memory footprints after warm-up and again after the final idle period. Check the agent with `leaks <pid>`, the logs and crash reports for zero crashes, restarts or panics, and the stream counters for no accumulating failures or abandoned sessions. Require zero reported leaks and no sustained memory growth or high idle CPU. A missing log window or unavailable leak report leaves the corresponding check incomplete.

### Attended RAM checks

- [ ] **Physical 4-edge rotation.** On the live SSC profile above, detach the cover and hold top, left, bottom and right up in turn. Capture the tablet and upright test content with the C920. Match the physical edge, `piano-sensors-edge` and agent output with 0°, 90°, 180° and 270° read-back and the corresponding landscape or portrait bounds. Repeat near both sides of a diagonal, face-up and face-down, and require the last angle to hold in ambiguous or flat poses. Capture NativeGopDisplay swap dimensions and flip/copy counters where available.
- [ ] **Physical cover attach and detach.** Start from an automatic portrait angle and attach the cover in view of the C920. Require the real attach bit to become 1, request 10 to disable acceleration, the edge to clear, and the display to return to 0° and stay there while attached. Detach and hold the tablet left-edge-up or right-edge-up. Verify attach bit 0, a fresh SSC session and an accepted portrait edge, and no automatic request before the 1.5 s detach hold plus the 600 ms agent settle. Require the corresponding portrait read-back and upright C920 picture. Repeat on the opposite side.
- [ ] **Touch and display agreement.** After each displayed angle settles, tap four labeled inset corner targets and capture the touches on the C920. For physical panel positions TL, TR, BL, BR, expect `TL TR BL BR` at 0°, `TR BR TL BL` at 90°, `BR BL TR TL` at 180°, and `BL TL BR TR` at 270°. Record all 16 results and read `machdep.piano_touch_native` for native mode, panel axes and errors. Try a drag across a rotation and then a new touch after read-back. Record any jump, wrong target or lost contact with the display and input timestamps.
- [ ] **Restore.** Return the display to 0° and verify the picture and touch. On an injected boot, set `TestEdge` to 0 before restoring the display, then clear the temporary preference and return from RAM. Record each check against its profile and keep synthetic and physical results identifiable.
