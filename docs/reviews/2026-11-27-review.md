## Review notes

- **Disk M5.** Traced serial and queued admission, overlapping page ranges, stage quarantine, completion ownership and the mode, synchronize and power fences. New pages still reach the data and bitmap FUA path. Cached writes require every page to be present. Forced writes drain earlier work before the cache flush and their FUA writes. The UFS one-slot read preference can bypass each ordinary head once. The durability counters sample the disk and UFS under separate locks, so a live read under load can contain slightly different sampling epochs. Host tests check policy and modeled ordering. UFS power-loss behavior remains a hardware qualification item. [Direct admission](../../package/NativeRamBlock/ConcurrentDirect.h) · [UFS slot selection](../../package/NativeUfs/RootCommands.h) · [Durability harness](../../tooling/native/piano-durability/test_durability.py)
- **Display.** Reviewed the Night Shift reference inversion and restoration, clock gate and stability checks, M5 register-range guards and GOP screenshot `EBUSY` exits. The clock reader and read-only snapshot keep distinct RPMh behavior. Report-only access still depends on the sampled hardware remaining clocked for the load. The Night Shift ABI has no enable bit, so a warm startup can still supply a warm reference. Both limitations remain in the opt-in qualification paths. [Night Shift](../../package/PianoDpu/DpuNightShift.h) · [Clock reader](../../package/PianoDpu/DpuClockRead.h) · [M5 snapshot](../../package/PianoDpu/DpuSnapshot.h) · [GOP diagnostics](../../package/NativeGopDisplay/GopShotDiagnostics.h)
- **Boot line.** Verified the 999-byte daily budget, the inclusive 1008-byte opt-in loader budget and 4095-byte merge, with the selector, separator and CPU suffix included. Covered the 4096-byte refusal, legacy-reader placement and the ESP diagnostic entry's extra reset arguments. [Length fixtures](../../tooling/adt/test-boot-args-length.py) · [ESP split fixtures](../../tooling/k273/test-esp-split.py)
- **Userland.** The camera check bounds image decoding, distinguishes refused launch from an uncertain launch and attempts app and movie cleanup after interruption. The microphone analyzer bounds RIFF chunks and rejects partial PCM frames. Metal cancellation remains conditional on SSH reaching the remote stop probe. The native benchmark retains its own duration limit. [Camera action](../../tooling/native/piano-cam-apps-check/action.py) · [Microphone analyzer](../../tooling/native/piano-mic-check/analyze.py) · [Metal poller](../../tooling/native/piano-metal-sustain/installed.spec)
