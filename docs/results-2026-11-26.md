## 47-bit user address space (RAM)

Kernel patch 0075 gives processes a 47-bit user address space. On RAM (uva47-qual2) it passed 32 checks: the full 1344 GiB
reservation succeeds, 200 of 200 mapping probes, pages near the top of the space map, write and read back correctly, copy-on-write
across fork works, the shared cache, malloc and the commpage are unaffected, and 2000 fork/exec rounds run clean. The one FAIL (a
Safari H.264 1080p run with 1 dropped frame of 600) is RAM-boot noise: the control boot of the same image with the change off
showed 600/0 and all its checks passed. 0075 goes into the next daily build, which is expected to bring Chrome's WebGPU.

## Internal storage (first numbers)

The first disk measurement on the installed system, on the Data volume, compared with Android on the same unit:

| Test | macOS | Android | Ratio |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sequential write, 4 MiB | 519 MB/s | 2137 MB/s | 0.24 |
| Sequential read, 4 MiB | 1435 MB/s | 4084 MB/s | 0.35 |
| 4K random read, queue depth 1 | 15580 IOPS | 14967 IOPS | 1.04 |
| 4K random write, queue depth 1 | 6494 IOPS | 36693 IOPS | 0.18 |
| 4K random read, 8 in flight | 15696 IOPS | 88663 IOPS | 0.18 |

- Reads on the Data volume do not scale with queue depth (8 in flight / 1 in flight = 1.08), while raw reads of the root window
  do (8.77), so the limit is in the Data stack, not in the UFS link.
- A 4K reader drops from 14490 to 1300 IOPS (p99 from 85 us to 2.3 ms) while a 1 MiB writer runs.
- UFS WriteBooster is already on from the boot firmware, so it is not the cause. The link runs at Gear 5, 2 lanes.
- Fixes are in progress.

### Night Shift and panel sleep

- **Night Shift (RAM boot, v13.1y12s):** the blue tint came from transposed cross terms in the PCC colour matrix and negative
  coefficients written in sign-magnitude form where the hardware expects two's complement. With both corrected, Night Shift
  was visibly orange on the tablet. With Night Shift off on the same RAM boot, the panel still looked warm. CoreBrightness
  sends a linear-light white-point matrix at boot, and applying it to encoded pixels exaggerates the tint. The `ns-base` fix
  passed host tests. Its tablet RAM-boot check and installation of the Night Shift fixes are pending.
- **M5 panel sleep (RAM boots):** v13.1y12r did not exercise M5 because the display AHB vote was off and the slice worker did
  not start. On v13.1y12s, the logs reported 9 of 10 master-first sleeps and 9 of 10 bonded wakes as OK, but the panel stayed
  dark in all three post-wake webcam stills. Analysis of the dark panel is open and installed enablement is pending.

### Fixed 144 Hz and 120 Hz frame info

- **144 Hz (RAM boot, `m120c-144`):** with the MM0 bandwidth vote, both links verified at **144.146 Hz**, with no interface
  underruns across the boot or during the motion tests. The app ran at 52.1 callbacks/s at load 0 and 48.4/s at load 64,
  with 42.7 and 34.7 distinct new-surface latches/s respectively.
