Updated 2026-11-19. Goal status is active. The current objective remains deployment and local qualification of native macOS 27.3 while preserving HyperOS 3.0.309 and validating both switch directions and recovery.

## Current state

- **Upstream:** latest `main` equals requested baseline `2438556c77c90c22c7b76e57eb8b40c44b35f1af`; no newer commits to mix in. The complete source pins are unchanged.
- **Tablet:** read-only ADB audit confirms `piano`, `SM8750P`, `OS3.0.309.0.WPYCNXM`, Android 16, unlocked bootloader, root available, active slot A. No device writes or reboot occurred.
- **Layout:** all six UFS LUN primary/backup GPT CRCs pass and their entry arrays match. First/last 1 MiB captures from all six LUNs are stored on E: and F:; SHA-256 and offline GPT CRC/array checks pass. These are GPT boundary backups, not disk images. Stock `userdata` fills LUN 0 through its last usable LBA; native `linux_rootfs`, `linux_data`, and ESP are absent. The A-slot boot triplet and `misc` have length-checked, SHA-256-matched copies on E: and F:, with fresh device rereads matching. Read-only Fastboot reported A current, both slots successful, retries 6/6, and partition sizes matching the captures. Normal reboot returned to HyperOS Android 16 build `BP2A.250605.031.A3`, slot A. This does not qualify full recovery.
- **Recovery:** one historical external backup exists and is preserved. Its separate DCIM and Pictures archives parse (5519 and 571 entries), but are not file-by-file compared with the tablet. The 52.8 GB `data-data.tar` begins with a 39-byte tar warning; after skipping that prefix, a read-only parser found 4,719 entries and 169,597,666 declared payload bytes before a tar end marker, while 52.6 GB remains unexplained and includes tar diagnostics. This is not a verified full archive or a verified 3.0.309 recovery set. Do not remove it or treat it as the only recovery copy.

Current device GPT has no `linux_rootfs`, `linux_data`, or ESP, so this is historical, not current qualification; how the device returned to stock Android is unknown. Its Git database lacks the pinned `2438556` commit and its lockfiles differ. Sibling checkout `piano-macos-latest` has 6.25 GB of k27/RAM build outputs and limited generated inputs, but no k27 source tree, persistent root or ESP; its lockfiles also differ. CLT clang 17 is available through `DEVELOPER_DIR`, but Python is 3.9.6 (the repo requires 3.11+), and NASM/IASL are missing. No build has started.
